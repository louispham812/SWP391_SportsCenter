01 • Project Introduction

Context & Operational Pain Points

Why traditional multi-sport facilities struggle to scale across 10+ sports

Market Context

Booming Demand for Multi-Sport Complexes

Modern sports centers no longer offer just one sport. Facilities like F Sport Center operate 16 courts across 10 sports (Badminton, Pickleball, Tennis, Football, Swimming, Gym, Yoga, etc.) serving hundreds of daily walk-in and online members.

Core Operational Complexity
Hourly Court Slots 1-on-1 PT & Group Classes Quota Packages Equipment Inventory Counter POS
01. Slot Conflicts & Double Booking

Manual booking via phone/Zalo lacks real-time slot locking, leading to overlapping reservations and unpaid no-shows.

02. Fragmented Membership Quotas

Hard to track remaining free hours/sessions across VIP, PT, and Class packages or combine quotas with voucher discounts.

03. Manual POS & Equipment Loss

Receptionists lack unified QR/RFID check-in, K80 receipt printing, and real-time tracking of rented rackets, balls, and shoes.

04. Reactive Maintenance & PT Tracking

Sudden court maintenance fails to auto-lock slots or notify affected bookings; coach attendance and commissions are calculated manually.

Self-Service

Counter POS

Scenario 1: Online Court Booking & Quota / VNPay Checkout

Actor: Member (member@sport.vn) • Goal: Book a Pickleball/Badminton slot without conflict

STEP 01

Discover & Filter

Member selects sport category (e.g., Pickleball / Badminton), date, and views real-time availability across 16 courts.

GET /api/v1/courts
STEP 02

10-Min Slot Hold

Selecting a time slot creates a temporary reservation lock for 10 minutes so no other user can double-book during checkout.

Status: PENDING_PAYMENT
STEP 03

Quota vs. VNPay

System checks active VIP package quota first; if covered, deducts hours automatically, or redirects to VNPay Sandbox with voucher support.

HMAC-SHA512 Signed
STEP 04

Instant Confirmation

Upon VNPay return callback or quota deduction, booking transitions to CONFIRMED and generates a unique Digital QR Pass.

GET /vnpay-return
STEP 05

Cancel / Refund Flow

If cancelled before policy cutoff, member submits a refund/quota restoration request routed directly to Manager Approval Center.

Audit Logged
🛡️ Concurrency Safeguard: Scheduled background job automatically releases unpaid locks after 10 minutes. Result: 0% Overlapping Bookings

05 • Conclusion

Project Outcomes, Limitations & Future Roadmap

Evaluating engineering deliverables and next steps

🏆 Key Outcomes Achieved

  • ✔️ Complete Full-Stack Delivery: 4 role-based portals backed by 31 normalized SQL Server tables and RESTful Spring Boot 4.1.2 APIs.
  • ✔️ Solved Real Operational Bottlenecks: Eliminated double-booking via 10-minute slot locking and automated Membership Quota vs. VNPay checkout.
  • ✔️ Unified O2O Experience: Connected online bookings with front-desk QR/RFID check-in, K80 thermal receipt printing, and equipment inventory tracking.
  • ✔️ High Engineering Quality: Verified business rules and user journeys using JUnit 5 unit tests and Playwright E2E automated browser tests.

⚠️ Current Limitations

  • • Sandbox Payment Gateway: Online transactions currently run on VNPay Sandbox rather than live merchant production credentials.
  • • Hardware Simulation: QR/RFID check-in operates via web interface without physical turnstile gate or IoT smart-locker integration.
  • • Web-Only Client: Responsive web app, but lacks a dedicated native iOS/Android mobile app with background push notifications.

🚀 Future Enhancements

  • • Build a React Native Mobile App with Firebase Push Notifications for upcoming court & PT reminders.
  • • Integrate IoT Smart Turnstiles & Automated Court Lighting triggered directly by confirmed booking time slots.
  • • Upgrade AI Fitness Assistant with computer-vision posture analysis for PT homework verification.
